# Portfolio surface brief

Scope: Home, Work, About, Skills, Contact, Privacy and all five `/work/[slug]` case-study routes. Visitor mode moves from Experience/Persuade on Home to Inspect/Read in the evidence passages. Hiring teams and founders should understand a concrete contribution, inspect a mechanism, then open the CV or contact Ayodeji.

Authority: user-pinned, code-led Possibility Engine direction; the emitted contract in `src/app/layout.tsx` and `REBUILD-PLAN.md` establish it. No approved image comp exists. `DESIGN.md` captures the built global world; this brief records each surface's job.

Memorable moment: the architectural headline meets a slowly changing impossible ribbon structure. Project selection connects this spectacle to named products; it does not turn the sculpture into a mandatory route selector or claim spatial page-transition choreography that is not built.

Constraints: keyboard and touch access, Pause/Resume, reduced motion, offscreen/hidden suspension, CSS graphics fallback and no-JavaScript navigation. Do not obscure the headline at tablet widths or hide evidence labels on mobile. Keep synthetic/illustrative labels next to demonstrations. Preserve the eight existing shipping rasters and their `.jpg.json` provenance sidecars; no generated raster imagery was added.

Verification handoff: `.impeccable/review/final-review.md` gives a Ship disposition after resolving the tablet collision and hidden experiment labels. This documentation pass extracted source and read the final review; it did not independently rerun browser tests. Local lab performance is not field or modest-phone performance proof.
