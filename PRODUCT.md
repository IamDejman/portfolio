## Product Principles

- Lead with working mechanisms and concrete contributions.
- Group related repositories into product families rather than inflating project counts.
- Keep the route from curiosity to credible proof short.
- Use synthetic or sanitized demonstration material; private source access is not permission to publish private data.
- Label reconstructions and prototypes honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve readable semantic content, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion behavior and a complete experience when advanced graphics are unavailable.
