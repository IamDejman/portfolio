# Portfolio surface brief

Scope: Home, Work, About, Skills, Contact, Privacy and all four `/work/[slug]` case-study routes. Visitor mode moves from Experience/Persuade on Home to Inspect/Read in the evidence passages. Hiring teams and founders should understand a concrete contribution, inspect a mechanism, then open the CV or contact Ayodeji.

Authority: user-pinned, code-led Possibility Engine direction; the emitted contract in `src/app/layout.tsx` and `REBUILD-PLAN.md` establish it. No approved image comp exists. `DESIGN.md` captures the built global world; this brief records each surface's job.

| Surface | Built composition and visitor action |
| --- | --- |
| Home `/` | Large “Ideas are only the beginning.” lettering and intersecting ribbon sculpture; project selection changes the sculpture and a direct detail link. Selected work, synthetic Skilladder mechanism, experiments with visible evidence labels, and a large contact close follow. |
| Work `/work` | Products Ayodeji built lead: Skilladder and Orderflow as engineering case studies, then GRWTEE, Banyan Claims and Harvesters. The Onboarding Engine and Tellerpoint case studies, a short Earlier product roles list (Carbin, GPS, Switch, BetaCare) and the other builds follow. |
| Case studies `/work/[slug]` | Every study tells its story in one order, numbered: the problem, what was built, the stack, one hard bug or tradeoff, the result, and where to see it. A part is left out only when there is nothing true to put in it. |
| Skilladder `/work/skilladder` | Case-study introduction and contribution metadata, the six-part story with its user-confirmed outcome panel and product capture, Under the hood, the context/grading/review/delivery walkthrough and the live grader. |
| Orderflow `/work/orderflow` | Shared commerce narrative and blue input/core/output diagram with vertical and delayed-payment controls. Illustrative state changes do not execute payments. |
| Onboarding `/work/onboarding-engine` | Agent stages with a synthetic scratchpad, outline, guide and expandable source material. Implemented imports remain separate from planned direct connectors. |
| Tellerpoint `/work/tellerpoint` | Shared readable case-study shell for a product delivery role, with no stack part because it was not a coding role; no fabricated interactive mechanisms are added. `/work/carbin` redirects to Earlier product roles on Work. |
| About `/about` | Grayscale retained portrait opposite personal narrative, followed by experience and background sections; mobile stacks portrait and text. |
| Skills `/skills` | Capability statements link to evidence, followed by detailed skill groups and working method. |
| Contact `/contact` | Blue opening field with yellow email action, direct contact rows and collaboration context. |
| Privacy `/privacy` | Preserved readable policy content inside the shared visual shell. |
| Error/not-found | Direct recovery actions and readable messages inherit the same typography, colors and chrome. |

Memorable moment: the architectural headline rises word by word as the impossible ribbon structure unfolds, and the ribbons spread apart as the visitor scrolls on. Project selection connects this spectacle to named products; it does not turn the sculpture into a mandatory route selector. Route changes are short view transitions, and a clicked project card's logo travels into its case study; the transition is a lift and rise, not spatial choreography between the sculpture and the detail view.

Constraints: keyboard and touch access, the footer Pause motion control, reduced motion, offscreen/hidden suspension, CSS graphics fallback and no-JavaScript navigation, with every page complete and readable when motion is off. Do not obscure the headline at tablet widths or hide evidence labels on mobile. Keep synthetic/illustrative labels next to demonstrations. Preserve the eight existing shipping rasters and their `.jpg.json` provenance sidecars; no generated raster imagery was added.

Verification handoff: `.impeccable/review/final-review.md` gives a Ship disposition after resolving the tablet collision and hidden experiment labels. The reported full-results.json covers 23 route/viewport checks and functional controls; targeted responsive results cover 390, 768, 1000 and 1440px. This documentation pass extracted source and read the final review; it did not independently rerun browser tests. Local lab performance is not field or modest-phone performance proof.

Unresolved publication work: user review and deployment. Real recorded agent runs and further live-product evidence remain future enrichment, not current claims. No design-direction decision is left open by this handoff.
